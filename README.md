# Customer Segmentation Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes customer performance by segmenting customers based on Revenue and Profit thresholds. The goal is to identify high-value customers, loss-making segments, and optimization opportunities.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Excel Formulas
- Data Visualization (Bubble Chart, KPIs)

## Key Metrics
- Revenue
- Customer Profit
- Profit Margin
- Return Rate

## Segmentation Logic
Customers are classified into four segments:
- High Revenue – High Profit
- High Revenue – Low Profit
- Low Revenue – High Profit
- Low Revenue – Low Profit

Thresholds are calculated using dataset averages.

## Dashboard
The dashboard summarizes:
- Revenue and profit by segment
- Profitability patterns
- Strategic focus areas

![Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Business Insights
- High Revenue–High Profit customers are the main profit drivers
- Low Revenue–Low Profit customers contribute volume but dilute margins
- Certain low-revenue customers generate disproportionately high margins
